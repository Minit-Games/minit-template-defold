Defold Minit Template — third party notices
===========================================

All artwork and all sound effects are generated from source at build time
(tools/gen-art.mjs and tools/gen-audio.mjs). The only third-party media is the
background music track below.


Background music
----------------
"Title Screen" from "5 Chiptunes (Action)" by Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio).
https://opengameart.org/content/5-chiptunes-action

Released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). From the author's
INFO.txt shipped with the pack: "These music tracks have been released under
CC0 creative commons license. You can do anything you want with these tunes."

CC0 requires no attribution; the credit here and in meta.json is given anyway.

The bundled file is a derivative: downmixed to mono and resampled to 16 kHz
(tools/gen-music.mjs). The original is 44.1 kHz stereo.
